How Deletion Works

Because SecureVault employs an end-to-end zero-knowledge architecture, your master password and vault encryption keys are never stored on our servers. When an account deletion is initiated, the server purges all associated records permanently:


  • Account Credentials: Google account identifier, linked email, and verification metadata.
  • Device Bindings & Sessions: All registered device public keys, active session tokens, and cryptographic attestation records.
  • Encrypted Vault Records: All ciphertext entries (passwords, secure notes, TOTP secrets, payment items).
  • Document Blobs: All encrypted scanned documents, image pages, and PDF artifacts stored in private object storage.
  • Share Links & Backups: All active external share references and server-side encrypted backup snapshots.
Permanent & Irreversible: Once purged, data cannot be recovered by anyone, including the SecureVault engineering team. Ensure you have exported any critical records before proceeding.

In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the SecureVault Android application installed, you can delete your account immediately with step-up verification:


  1. Open SecureVault and unlock your vault.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Select Delete Account & Data and confirm with your biometric/PIN.
In-app deletion executes immediately and confirms zero active records remaining on the server within seconds.
